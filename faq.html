FAQ

Questions? We probably have an answer.

Everything you need to know before booking LOLA.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as possible for weekends and popular dates. Last-minute inquiries are still welcome.

How much space does the booth need?

Space depends on the experience; we confirm exact requirements before the event.

Is setup included?

Yes, setup and breakdown are included unless otherwise stated.

Can I customize photos or videos?

Yes. Templates, overlays, branding, and other visual elements can be customized depending on package.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel outside the primary service area may carry an additional fee.

Do you work with corporate clients?

Absolutely. LOLA can support conferences, launches, company events, and branded activations.
