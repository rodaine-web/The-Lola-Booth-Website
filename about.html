About LOLA

We like good events and even better photos.

The LOLA Booth was built around a simple idea: the best event experiences feel effortless in the moment and unforgettable afterward.

Our Story

More than a booth in the corner.

LOLA brings photography, design, entertainment, and hospitality together in one experience. We care about the way the booth looks in the room, how the lighting feels on real people, how smoothly guests move through it, and whether the final photo or video is something they actually want to keep.

That is why every part of the experience matters, from the first inquiry to setup, guest interaction, sharing, printing, and the final gallery. The technology should work. The design should feel intentional. The people should feel taken care of.

Whether LOLA is at a wedding, birthday, shower, graduation, corporate event, or brand activation, the goal stays the same: create a moment people enjoy while they are in it and remember after it is over.

What We Believe

Good experiences are built on the details.

These are the principles behind how LOLA looks, feels, and shows up at every event.

01

People First

The technology matters. The people matter more. Guests should feel comfortable, welcomed, and excited to participate.

02

Beautifully Curated

From booth design to overlays, lighting, backdrops, and signage, every visual detail should feel intentional.

03

Easy From Start to Finish

Booking a photo booth should not become another planning headache. We keep the process clear, organized, and responsive.

04

Worth Keeping

We want to create photos, videos, and messages people actually save, share, print, replay, and come back to.

The LOLA Standard

What you should expect every time.

A premium experience is not just the equipment. It is how everything comes together before, during, and after the event.

01

A setup that belongs in the room

Clean equipment, thoughtful presentation, and a footprint designed to complement the event instead of distracting from it.

02

Flattering, intentional lighting

Lighting is part of the experience. We want guests to look like themselves, just on a very good day.

03

Attendants who understand hospitality

Our role is not just to watch the equipment. It is to help guests, keep things moving, and make the experience feel easy.

04

Creative that feels personal

Overlays, welcome screens, signage, magazine covers, and branded elements should feel designed for the event, not dropped into a template.

05

A clean handoff after the event

Digital galleries, videos, prints, and Audio Guest Book recordings should be easy to receive, revisit, and share.
